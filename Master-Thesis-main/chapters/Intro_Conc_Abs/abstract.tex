\chapter*{Abstract}

This thesis presents the development and characterization of the experimental system\CCthesis{l'equilibrio fra le parti}{Conteggio delle parole, note escluse: introduzione, abstract e conclusioni 956; capitolo 1 (teoria) 9817; capitolo 2 (apparato e metodi) 13161; capitolo 3 (risultati) 6502. To counteract potential heating during optical transport, Electromagnetically Induced Transparency (EIT) cooling was investigated numerically via a quantum master-equation model in QuTiP. These results establish the main experimental and numerical prerequisites for a future implementation of conveyor-belt transport and sub-Doppler cooling of cold atoms inside the HCPCF.
